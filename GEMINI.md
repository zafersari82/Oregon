# Gemini Instructions for Oregon

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `docs/architecture/OREGON_ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md`
3. `docs/architecture/OREGON_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACT.md`
